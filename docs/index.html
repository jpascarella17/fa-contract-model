What stands out

  • Length is off in eight of the eleven in-scope deals, and six of those signed longer than projected. Skubal signed for 9 years against a projected 5, Peralta and Rogers for 5 against 3, Bauers for 4 against 2, Phillips for 3 against 1, and May for 3 against 1. Pagán (1 year against 2) and Bubic (1 against 2) went the other way. Gray, Loáisiga and Goldschmidt matched.
  • AAV is close for five of the eleven in-scope deals. Pagán matched the projection exactly at $11.5M a year, Loáisiga and Goldschmidt were within $1M, and Bauers and Phillips were within $2.5M. The other six all signed for more than projected: Skubal at about $50M against $32.2M, Gray at $27.5M against $16.9M, May at $17.5M against $10.3M, Peralta at about $25M against $19.3M, Rogers at about $24M against $18.2M, and Bubic at $17.5M against $14.4M. Nine of the eleven signed above the projection. The model flagged Skubal's projection as a floor.
  • Small relief deals come in below the projection. Webb, Mayza and Weiss were projected at $6.7M to $8.2M and signed for $1.85M to $2.75M. Thielbar was projected at $5.7M and signed for $3.25M. Hill, projected at $3.1M, signed for $2.25M, and Suter, projected at $4.9M, signed for $4M.
  • Both qualifying offer acceptances landed above the projection. Jeffers took $23.15M against a projected $18.1M a year, and Bednar took the same against $12.8M. Those salaries are set by formula, so they are not scored.

How to read this

  • In scope means a contract worth at least $5M in total. The model trains only on deals that size, so smaller deals are shown here but fall outside what it was built to predict.
  • Projected total is projected AAV times whole projected years.
  • Qualifying offer acceptances take a one-year salary set by formula ($23.15M this year). They are shown but not scored, and the model's training data excludes them.
  • Eleven in-scope signings are a small, early sample, so treat any pattern as tentative. No average is shown yet. Gray's $27.5M a year is the reported $55M split evenly over two years, and his deal includes an opt-out whose terms are not public. Loáisiga's deal is pending a physical and has a third-year team option for 2029 that is not counted.

Notes. Reported means a news report of an agreement that is not yet official. Accepted means the player accepted a qualifying offer by the deadline. Weiss: guaranteed terms only; his deal also includes a 2028 team option and performance bonuses. Skubal: the reported $450M over nine years split evenly, about $50M a year, with a $45M signing bonus and no opt-outs. Goldschmidt: base salary only, plus up to $2M in bonuses. Suter: a $3.5M club option for 2028 with a $250K buyout is not counted. Peralta and Rogers: the reported totals ($125M and $120M over five years) split evenly. Japanese players who signed from Japan's league are not covered.
