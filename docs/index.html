What stands out

  • Length is the larger miss. Bauers signed for 4 years against a projected 2, Phillips for 3 against 1, and May for 3 against 1. Pagán went the other way, 1 year against 2.
  • AAV is close for three of the four in-scope deals, each within $2.5M of the projection. Dustin May is the exception: $17.5M a year against a projected $10.3M.
  • Small relief deals come in far below the projection. Webb, Mayza and Weiss were projected at $6.7M to $8.2M and signed for $1.85M to $2.75M. Hill, projected at $3.1M, signed for $2.25M.
  • Both qualifying offer acceptances landed above the projection. Jeffers took $23.15M against a projected $18.1M a year, and Bednar took the same against $12.8M. Those salaries are set by formula, so they are not scored.

How to read this

  • In scope means a contract worth at least $5M in total. The model trains only on deals that size, so smaller deals are shown here but fall outside what it was built to predict.
  • Projected total is projected AAV times whole projected years.
  • Qualifying offer acceptances take a one-year salary set by formula ($23.15M this year). They are shown but not scored, and the model's training data excludes them.
  • Four in-scope signings are too few to judge accuracy, so no average is shown.

Notes. Reported means a news report of an agreement that is not yet official. Accepted means the player accepted a qualifying offer by the deadline. Weiss: guaranteed terms only; his deal also includes a 2028 team option and performance bonuses.
